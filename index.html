§Bio

My interests include algebraic and geometric aspects of quantum information and post-quantum cryptography, particularly nonlocal games, quantum complexity, and lattice-based cryptography. I recently completed an M.Sc. in Mathematics and Statistics at the University of Ottawa. Previously, I did my undergrad in Theoretical Physics at the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK). I am very fortunate to have been advised and mentored by Prof. A. Bogdanov, Prof. P. M. Hui, Prof. A. Mehta, and Prof. M. Nevins.
